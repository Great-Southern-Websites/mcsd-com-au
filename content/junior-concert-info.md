## Dress rehearsal

<ul class="info-list">
<li><strong>Date</strong>Tuesday 1 December 2026</li>
<li><strong>Location</strong>Peninsula Baptist Church (Mount Martha Centre / New Peninsula Centre), 370 Craigie Rd, Mount Martha VIC 3934</li>
<li><strong>Time</strong>Baby Ballet only: 4.30pm to 5.15pm<br>Kinder and Pre-Primary: 5.00pm to 6.45pm</li>

- Arrive in full costume.
- Hair in a ballet bun (or 2 buns if hair is short).
- Parent drop-off and pick-up: drop off at the Flinders kinder entrance and pick up at the same place. Students will be released one class at a time by Miss Bec (Monday Kinder 3.45), Miss Teaghan (Wednesday Kinder 10.30 and Wednesday Pre-Primary 11.00) and Miss Catherine (Tuesday Kinder 3.30 and Saturday Kinder 9.00).

## Baby Ballet Wednesday 9.15 and 10.00 class information

**Dress rehearsal on Tuesday 1 December 2026**

- Starting time: 4.30pm
- Finishing time: 5.15pm

Only one adult may accompany each Baby Ballet student. This is the adult who will be performing on stage with them. The performing adult should dress in all black. For the safety of all our little performers, please do not bring younger or older siblings to this dress rehearsal.

**Concert on Saturday 5 December 2026**

Each Baby Ballet student has 2 reserved front-row seats, one for them and one for the adult dancing with them. They will go on stage for their dance, then return to their seats. Family and friends can purchase as many tickets as they like.
