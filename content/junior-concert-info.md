- Arrive in full costume.
- Hair in a ballet bun (or 2 buns if hair is short).

**Parent drop-off and pick-up:** drop off at the Flinders kinder entrance and pick up at the same place. Students will be released one class at a time:

- Miss Bec: Monday Kinder 3.45
- Miss Teaghan: Wednesday Kinder 10.30 and Wednesday Pre-Primary 11.00
- Miss Catherine: Tuesday Kinder 3.30 and Saturday Kinder 9.00

## Baby Ballet (Wednesday 9.15 and 10.00) class information

**Dress rehearsal on Tuesday 1 December:** starting time 4:30 pm, finishing time 5:15 pm.

Only one adult may accompany each Baby Ballet student: this is the adult who will be performing on stage with them. The performing adult should dress in all black. For the safety of all our little performers, please do not bring younger or older siblings to this dress rehearsal.

**Concert on Saturday 5 December:** each Baby Ballet student has 2 reserved front-row seats, one for them and one for the adult dancing with them. They will go on stage for their dance, then return to their seats. Family and friends can purchase as many tickets as they like.

## What to wear to the dress rehearsal and concert

- **Tights:** theatrical pink or ballet pink footed tights.
- **Shoes:** pink ballet shoes (name on inside).
- **Hair:** worn in a ballet bun with a hair net and bobby pins.
- **Makeup:** light neutral makeup of your own choosing (optional).
- **Tan bodysuit under costume:** mandatory as per child safety. Children do not get fully undressed backstage.
- **Costume:** provided costume (name on inside).
- **Dressing gown:** any colour dressing gown over the top of the costume (name on inside).
