## Enrolments for 2026 are now closed

Thank you for the wonderful interest in MCSD.

Re-enrolments for our current students will take place towards the end of 2026, giving our existing dancers the opportunity to secure their place for the new year.

Any remaining places will then open to new students for 2027, subject to class availability.

2027 enrolment information and available classes will be published here in January.
